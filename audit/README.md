## Evidence rules

- `VERIFIED IN BROWSER` is **not used anywhere**: no browser could be installed in this environment.
- Engine claims are `VERIFIED BY EXECUTABLE TEST` against real encoders and a real PDF engine.
- No keyword volumes, traffic, rankings, market share, conversion rates or customer-complaint statistics are invented. Where such data was needed, the report says so.
- Competitor facts carry the URL and the observation date.
